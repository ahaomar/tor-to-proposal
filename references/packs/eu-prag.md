# EU PRAG pack (EuropeAid / NEAR / INTPA service & works contracts)

## Where things live
- Solicitations: EU Funding & Tenders portal (ec.europa.eu/eurostat beyond —
  use the Funding & Tenders portal search); EuropeAid procurement.
- Rules: PRAG — Practical Guide for contract award (international
  cooperation); the document is authoritative, edition-sensitive, and free
  online. ALWAYS check the PRAG edition named in the ToR.

## Structure conventions to verify
- Service contracts: full procedure with technical offer + financial offer
  sealed separately; shortlisting stages for restricted procedures.
- Evaluation grids published in the ToR annexes — often the most detailed of
  any donor (organization, methodology, key experts, proven experience).
  Extract sub-elements verbatim into sub-elements.json for the simulator.
- Key experts: the grid scores named experts' qualifications — a firm bid
  lives or dies on CV-grid fit (run cv-gap per expert).

## Financial side
- Price schedule per deliverable or per expert-day; VAT rules by contract
  type (international services often zero-rated, local services not).
- Global pricing rules in the instructions to tenderers; budget caps stated.

## Watch for
- Eligibility rules (origin of tenderer, nationality of experts) are strict
  and disqualifying.
- Declaration on honour + exclusion criteria forms required at submission.
- Clarification deadlines and a public Q&A published as an annex — check for
  addenda before finalizing; they change grids.
