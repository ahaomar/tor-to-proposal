# World Bank / IFI pack

## Where things live
- Solicitations: World Bank eProcurement / vendor portal (worldbank.org);
  consultant opportunities under STC (Short-Term Consultant) and firm CQS/RFQ
  tracks.
- Rules: Procurement Regulations and Consultant Guidance (pubdocs.worldbank.org).
- Vendor registration: World Bank Supplier Portal (needed for contracting).

## STC (individual consultant) conventions
- Daily rate + business travel expenses quoted separately; STC maximum daily
  rate schedule exists — the effective ceiling changes by fiscal year: verify
  the current schedule and cite it (source + date) in market-rates-research.
- Evaluation: CV against stated criteria; sometimes a short technical
  response. Extract the actual criteria; never assume the common
  education/experience/language sketch.

## Firm consultations (CQS/RFQ/RFP)
- Budget caps are often pre-announced — price discovery is partly given;
  position still matters for the technical/financial mix in RFPs.

## Watch for
- COI disclosure requirements (relatives at the Bank, prior Bank employment).
- "Not to exceed X days" ceilings: pricing beyond them is at your risk.
- Deliverable-based STCs: milestone table drives payment; mirror it in the
  technical response.
