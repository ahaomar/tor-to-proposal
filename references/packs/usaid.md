# USAID pack

## Where things live
- Solicitations: sam.gov (US federal single portal) for direct contracts;
  BAA/RFAs on grants.gov; much USAID consultancy flows through implementers
  (Chemonics, DAI, FHI 360, RTI...) who subcontract with their own portals —
  identify WHICH track the ToR belongs to first.
- Rules: ADS 300s (acquisition/assistance policy), FAR/DFARS for contracts;
  implementers follow their own prime-award terms.

## Conventions to verify
- Direct contracts: evaluation per FAR 15.3 or best-value tradeoff — the
  ToR/RFQ states the significant factors and their order; mirror them.
- Subcontracts (most common for consultants): prime's template governs;
  NTE (not-to-exceed) rates; mark-up structures are the prime's, not yours.
- Personal services contractors (PSC): published salary bands (GS/FS
  equivalents) — a real, citable benchmark for market-rates-research.

## Financial side
- Rates commonly quoted all-inclusive daily; travel billed separately at
  actuals with caps (Fly America for US-origin travel).
- Payment terms of the prime flow down — verify payment reputation
  (dossier) before quoting tight contingency.

## Watch for
- US nationality/citizenship requirements for some PSCs.
- Organizational conflict-of-interest certifications.
- OFAC sanctions screening clauses — relevant if you work in sanctioned
  adjacent environments.
