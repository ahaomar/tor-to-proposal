# UNDP pack

Conventions to verify in the ToR — never assume; UNDP templates vary by
country office and year.

## Where things live
- Solicitations: UNGM (ungm.org) → "UNDP" + country; some IC/SSA posts also on
  the country-office site and jobs portals.
- Rules: UNDP Procurement Policy (pop.undp.org) — the ToR's GCCs derive from it.
- Vendor registration: UNGM vendor account (needed before award, not always
  before bidding — check the ToR's eligibility section, p.X).

## Typical structure of an individual-consultant (IC) solicitation
- Two-envelope: technical first; financial opened only above the technical
  threshold (commonly 70/30 technical/financial — VERIFY the split and the
  threshold in the ToR's evaluation section, p.X).
- Technical grid commonly covers education, relevant experience, methodology/
  approach, languages. Extract the actual grid with extract.mjs; do not reuse
  this sketch.

## Financial side
- Financial Proposal form is usually an Excel annex — template-filler `map`
  it; totals must remain the form's own formulas.
- IC contracts are output-based lump sums in most country offices; daily fee
  × estimated days appears in the form; check whether travel is included.

## Watch for
- "No deviation" GCC acceptance line — echo it verbatim in the cover letter.
- Clarification deadline is separate from submission deadline.
- Attested copies of degrees/diplomas sometimes required WITH the offer.
